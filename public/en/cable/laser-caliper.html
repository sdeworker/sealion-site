Dual-axis laser caliper

Diameter and ovality, measured on two axes without touching the product

Lasers scan on two axes at once, so nothing touches the product. Outer diameter and ovality are output live at ±0.001mm, at line speed, and the data can be handed to a PLC to close a loop.

Features

Two axes at once Measuring on both axes avoids the wandering reading a single-axis gauge gives on an oval product.
Non-contact Nothing touches the surface, which suits soft, hot or finish-critical products.
±0.001mm For lines held to tight dimensional tolerance.
High-speed in-line Continuous output at line speed, with no need to stop and sample.
PLC connectable Data can go to the line controller and take part in automatic correction.
Ovality as well Diameter and ovality together, so out-of-round shows as it happens.

Specification

Principle Dual-axis laser scanning, non-contact, outer diameter and ovality output in real time.
Features Dual-axis synchronisation, non-destructive, 1µm (0.001mm) resolution, high-speed in-line measurement, connects to a PLC.
Advantages Accurate and stable, holds up in harsh conditions, full inspection around the clock, replaces manual checking.
Value Higher pass rate and throughput, less scrap, dimensional control that runs itself.
