About Sealion

An industrial-control company for extrusion

Founded in 2008, Guangzhou Sealion Software Technology Co., Ltd. is a national high-tech enterprise integrating R&D, manufacturing and sales of industrial control systems. Our work spans hardware EDA, low-level DSP, ARM and host software, electrical-control design, PLC programming and mechanical design — from core algorithms to full-line solutions.

In-house development

Hardware EDA, low-level DSP, ARM and host software, electrical control, mechanical design — we walk the whole chain ourselves rather than integrating other people's boxes.

02

Core technology held

60 intellectual property registrations, all held by Sealion. The scanning box's auto-tracking probe and the laser diameter device each carry a patent number you can look up.

03

Low-level algorithms

PID closed loops, adaptive ratio control, DSP echo capture and noise-immune filtering. Whether a reading is right comes down to the algorithm, not a bought-in module.
