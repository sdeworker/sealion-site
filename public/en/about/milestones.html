Milestones

It started with one gravimetric control system

Founded in 2008. The first gravimetric control system in 2009, the first ultrasonic thickness gauge in 2010 — everything since has grown out of those two.

  1. 2025
    • Once again recognised as a High-Tech Enterprise
  2. 2023
    • The Pipe Inbound Quality & Safety System is named a Guangdong Province Excellent High-Tech Product
  3. 2022–2025
    • Awarded “Specialised & Innovative” enterprise status
  4. 2020–2021
    • Extrusion Cloud launched
    • The Pipe Inbound Quality & Safety System launched
  5. 2019
    • Re-certified as a High-Tech Enterprise
  6. 2018
    • The Gravimetric Control System is recognised as a Guangdong Province High-Tech Product
    • The Ultrasonic Online Thickness System is recognised as a Guangdong Province High-Tech Product
  7. 2017
    • ISO 9000 quality system certification
    • IP management system certification
  8. 2016
    • Awarded National High-Tech Enterprise status
  9. 2015
    • Certified as a software enterprise, with multiple software copyrights granted
    • Awarded “Sci-Tech Innovation Little Giant” enterprise status
  10. 2014
    • Set up Sealion's own machining workshop, to hold quality and delivery in our own hands
  11. 2013
    • The Guangzhou Sealion R&D centre is founded
    • East China, Southwest and North China offices established
  12. 2012
    • Sealion moves into Guangzhou Science City
  13. 2010
    • The first ultrasonic thickness measuring system is developed
  14. 2009
    • The first gravimetric control system is developed
  15. 2008
    • Guangzhou Sealion Software Technology Co., Ltd. is founded
