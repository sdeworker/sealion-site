Meltblown nonwoven

From dosed feeding to the spinneret — steady metering is what makes the fibre lay down evenly.

Meltblown fabric is made through dosed feeding, screw melting, melt filtration, metering-pump dosing, melt distribution and spinning at the spinneret.

Sealion's gravimetric metering makes the spinning more even and the extrusion more stable — steady the metering upstream and the fibre forming downstream has something to stand on.
