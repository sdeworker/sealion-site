Sheet & board extrusion

Several materials blended to an exact ratio — get the ratio wrong and the board won't be even.

Lines that need several materials blended to a precise ratio — plastic film, cable, pipe, sheet and compounding — are where Sealion's gravimetric dosing belongs.

The masterbatch weighing version meters each component by loss-in-weight to 4‰ accuracy, with each component controlled independently; together with gravimetric control steadying the draw-off, sheet thickness comes out more even.
