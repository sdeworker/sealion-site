Cable sheathing

The more complex the spec, the higher the repeatability demanded — and the tighter the sheath thickness has to be.

Wire and cable are made by drawing, stranding and sheathing. The more complex the spec, the higher the repeatability, and the more exacting the sheath's base thickness becomes.

Sealion's gravimetric control and ultrasonic gauging give the extrusion line an integrated smart-control solution. For small-diameter sheathing the SL-Ultrascan series applies — the manual names wires and cables among its uses, measuring wall 0.18–10mm on diameters of 2–50mm.
