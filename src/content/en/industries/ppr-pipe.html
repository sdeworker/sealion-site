PPR pipe

PE/PPR/PERT high-speed lines run up to 20 m/min — adjust the speed and the tube goes thick or thin, or breaks outright.

On today's popular PE/PPR/PERT high-speed lines, linear speed reaches 20 m/min. Adjusting that speed makes the tube too thick or too thin — sometimes it breaks.

The double-output gravimetric control system controls both extrusions from the line's twin haul-off speeds and host output: it automatically regulates screw speed, twin haul-off and feed rate in real time to match the set weight accuracy, holding weight-per-metre constant.
