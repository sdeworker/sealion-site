Notice: the 34th CHINAPLAS is postponed and relocated

2020-04

With the pandemic largely under control in China but still uncertain overseas, the 34th CHINAPLAS was postponed again.

It was rescheduled to 13–16 April 2021 and moved to the Shenzhen International Convention & Exhibition Center.
